## Description
Know what's happening in your store without opening the staff panel. Event Watcher posts FaxStore events to your Discord channels as they happen, and sends them to your phone through the Pushover app.

Install it from the App Store, pick an event, choose a channel and save. That's it.

### Ten events, each set up on its own

- **Store**: new releases, new orders and new reviews.
- **Billing**: invoices created, invoices paid, subscription renewal invoices and accepted quotes.
- **Community**: new accounts, form submissions and new blog posts.

Every event has its own Discord channel, role to mention and embed colour. Turn on the ones you want and leave the rest off.

View the full listing - [Event Watcher App](https://apps.faxstore.info/app/event-watcher)

### Release announcements your community will see

- Post every new release with its version, changelog and product image.
- **Product overrides**: give each product its own colour, update role or channel. Ping @Nimbus Updates for one product and post another in #announcements.
- Coming from Release Watcher? Your release channel and product settings are copied across automatically.

### Alerts for your team

- Ping your staff role when an order comes in or an invoice is paid.
- Away from your desk? Turn on **Send to Pushover** and get a push notification on your phone or desktop, with a link straight to the order, invoice or review.
- **Send a test** for any event to check it arrives before you save.

### Built for FaxStore

- Messages are posted by your own FaxStore Discord bot.
- Every message links back to the matching page on your store.
- Settings live in your own database, and the app creates its table when it starts.
- Free and open source, so you can see exactly what it does, or use it as a starting point for your own app.

## Requirements

- FaxStore, at least version 3.4.0
- The FaxStore [Discord Integration](https://docs.weblutions.com/spaces/faxstore/feat-discord), for Discord messages.
- Optional: the [Pushover app](https://apps.faxstore.info/app/pushover), for push notifications.

## Installing

1. In your FaxStore staff panel, open **Apps → Explore Apps**, find **Event Watcher** and click install.
2. Open **Event Watcher** from the Apps section of the staff panel sidebar.
3. Pick an event, turn on **Watch this event**, choose a channel and select **Save settings**.
